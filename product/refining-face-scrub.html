About the Product:

‍

Refining Face Scrub is crafted to provide a revitalizing skincare experience by eliminating impurities and promoting healthy skin renewal. Enriched with natural exfoliants like fine walnut shell powder and soothing botanical extracts, this scrub offers a perfect balance between effective cleansing and gentle care.

‍

It unclogs pores, smoothens rough patches, and prepares your skin for better absorption of moisturizers and treatments. Ideal for weekly use, it delivers a polished, refreshed look without causing irritation or dryness.

‍

  • Weight: 3 oz
  • Grams: 85 g
  • Milliliters: 85 ml
  • Skin Type: Suitable for Normal, Oily, Dry, and Combination Skin
  • Key Ingredients: Walnut Shell Powder, Aloe Vera, Vitamin C
  • Scent: Fresh Herbal Aroma

‍

Achieve soft, renewed skin with the Refining Face Scrub — your essential for a luminous, healthy complexion.

Key Benefits:

‍

  • Gently exfoliates to remove dead skin and impurities.
  • Helps unclog and minimize the appearance of pores.
  • Smooths rough, uneven skin texture for a polished finish.
  • Enhances skin’s natural glow and vibrancy.
  • Prepares skin for better absorption of skincare products.
  • Suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin when used gently.
  • Infused with soothing and nourishing natural extracts.

‍

How to Use:

‍

  1. Apply to Damp Skin: After cleansing, take a small amount onto your fingertips.
  2. Massage Gently: Use circular motions to exfoliate, avoiding the eye area.
  3. Rinse Thoroughly: Wash off with lukewarm water.
  4. Use 2-3 times a week, depending on your skin type and needs.

‍

Packaging & Storage:

‍

  • Container: Convenient squeeze tube for easy, hygienic use.
  • Storage Tip: Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.

‍

  • Dermatologist-Tested: Safe and gentle for regular use.
