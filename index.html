Answers to Your Queries

01. What is the 21-Type Color Analysis process, and how is it different?

Most conventional color systems limit analysis to 4 or 12 seasons, which often fails to capture subtle variations in undertone, depth, and chroma for diverse skin tones. The Colorist uses a patented 21-type seasonal color analysis process — the most detailed and only one of its kind in India. Instead of handing you a generic swatch card, we determine your precise best tones, sub-tones, worst tones, metals, and contrasts so you are never restricted in what you can wear.

02. Can I do a color analysis online, and is it as accurate as in-person?

We accept both walk-ins and appointments to make your experience as convenient as possible. While walk-ins are always welcome, booking an appointment in advance ensures you get your preferred time slot without any waiting.

03. What is included in a Body and Face Analysis consultation?

We use only premium, salon-quality products that are designed to nourish and protect your hair. Our carefully selected range includes top-tier shampoos, conditioners, styling gels, and beard oils to enhance your grooming routine and maintain a healthy look.

04. What deliverables do I receive after my session?

The ideal frequency for a haircut depends on your preferred style and hair type. For short, clean-cut styles, we recommend a trim every 3-4 weeks, while longer styles can go up to 6-8 weeks. Regular maintenance helps keep your look fresh and well-groomed.

05. Do you offer services for men?

Yes! Our professional beard grooming services include trimming, shaping, and conditioning to keep your beard looking stylish and well-maintained. Whether you prefer a clean, sculpted look or a full, well-defined beard, we tailor our services to suit your style.
